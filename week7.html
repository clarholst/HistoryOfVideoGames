How To Play

  • Type what you want to do and press Enter.
  • Short commands work: look, help, routes.
  • You do not need exact phrasing.
  • Try verbs: board, stay, ask, enter, say.
  • Use restart to begin a fresh run.

Useful Commands

  • help or ?
  • look or l
  • options
  • routes
  • stats
  • restart
